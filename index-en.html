Who are we?

Dedalis is a technical design office founded in 2026, specializing in large-scale additive manufacturing with cement-based materials.

The Dedalis team relies on 10 years of experience in this field, both computational and operational. From academic research to industrialization for construction sites, through the creation of artworks and the development of urban furniture, we master the technical challenges and have the network (printers, cement manufacturers, structural design offices, etc.) to help you successfully complete your projects.

Our mission: to make ambitious concepts technically feasible, whether for a complex, custom, one-of-a-kind object or for series production. Thanks to rigorous, responsive engineering connected to the state of the art, we master the challenges to deliver solutions tailored to your needs.

Who is it for?

  • Individuals, Artists, Designers
  • Project Owners and Project Managers
  • Construction Companies and Printers
  • Research Laboratories and R&D Cells

Tools & Methods

Dedalis relies on the same tools used by the various actors in the industry to facilitate exchanges and the integration of our work into our partners environments.

For complex cases or special requests, we develop custom tools that remain the property of our clients or become open-source.

Dedalis also contributes to the development of the industry by actively participating in the AFGC working groups dedicated to 3D concrete printing. This involvement guarantees constant technical monitoring, in-depth knowledge of the state of the art, and consistency with the emerging standards of the sector.
